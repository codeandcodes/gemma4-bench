Updated 2026-06-05 10:09:35

Key findings

  • In-scope intent accuracy is nearly quant-independent — only 91.5–91.8% across Q8_0 / IQ4_XS / IQ2_M. Even 2-bit holds for routing in-domain utterances.
  • Out-of-scope detection is what low-bit costs you — OOS recall 84% / 86% / 76%; IQ2_M drops ~9pt (it abstains less), pulling its overall accuracy down.
  • IQ4_XS is the sweet spot — matches Q8_0 accuracy, edges it on OOS recall, and runs ~18% faster (306 vs 260 tok/s aggregate).
  • Throughput scales inversely with precision — 260 → 306 → 334 tok/s aggregate (smaller quant = faster decode).
